How this works

Kiosk — put your laptop or tablet by the door, screen facing students. Switch which class period is active using the pills at the top. Students scan their ID with the camera (or type it if they forgot it) and pick a destination.

Teacher view — open in a separate browser tab. Same period selector, plus today's log, frequent late returns, auto-referrals, and student detail.

Search — finds any student across all your classes by name or partial ID. Useful for parent emails or AP referral conversations.

All data stays in your browser. Use Export CSV periodically to back up. If your school's ID barcodes encode something different from the visible ID number, the system will recognize the scan as "unknown" and let you map it to the right student — only needed once per kid.
